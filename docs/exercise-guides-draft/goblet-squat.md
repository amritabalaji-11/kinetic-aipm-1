## Top of page
- **One-line summary:** Hold one weight at your chest and sit straight down between your knees. It is the squat to learn first.
- **Level:** Beginner – Intermediate. **[CONFIRM]** ACE's library rates it "Intermediate"; the coach who created it (Dan John) designed it to teach beginners how to squat. Beginners should start with a light weight.
- **Equipment:** One dumbbell, or a kettlebell.
- **Main muscles:** Quads, glutes.
- **Helping muscles:** Hamstrings, inner thighs, core, upper back, forearms (they hold the weight).
- **Starting weight:** Start light. Pick a weight you could lift 12–15 times with good form, and add weight once 12 reps feel easy.

## Quick view (the "Next up" card, 15 seconds)
1. Big breath in, brace your core, hold the weight tight to your chest.
2. Sit down between your knees. Knees over your little toes, heels stay down.
3. Push the floor away through your whole foot. Chest and hips rise together.

- **Breathing:** Breathe in and brace at the top, stay tight on the way down, breathe out as you stand. **[CONFIRM]** Sources differ: some say breathe in going down and out coming up; others say brace and hold. This wording matches the app's AI coaching reference.
- **Tempo:** 2 seconds down, 1 second pause at the bottom, 1 second up.
- **Sets and reps:** 2–3 sets of 8–12.

## How to do it
**Setup**
1. Hold one dumbbell upright against your chest (or a kettlebell by the horns), elbows pointing down.
2. Stand with feet about shoulder-width apart, toes turned out slightly.
3. Take a big breath in and brace your core before you go down.
4. Spread the floor apart with your feet, keeping your whole foot planted.
5. Chest tall. Keep the weight at chest height the whole time.
6. Eyes forward, or slightly down at a spot on the floor a few steps ahead. Keep your neck long, in line with your spine. **[CONFIRM]** This head and neck position comes from general coaching practice, not from one of the sources listed below. Please have a trainer check it.

**Movement**
1. Sit down and slightly back, bending your hips and knees together. Keep your elbows close to your ribs.
2. Push your knees out over your little toes as you go down.
3. Lower as far as you can while keeping your heels down and your back flat. **[CONFIRM]** the target depth is hips at or just below knee height (ACE: "until the hips are below the knees"; other sources say thighs parallel). Make clear depth is a goal, not a must.
4. Pause for one second at the bottom, then push both feet into the floor and stand. Chest and hips rise together.

## Common mistakes
| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| Heels lift off the floor | Warm up your ankles first, or try a small plate under your heels. |
| Knees cave inward | Squeeze your glutes and push your knees out over your little toes. |
| Weight drifts down to your belly, chest drops | Keep it at chest height and think "chest up". |
| Dropping fast, or only going down a little | Count two seconds down and pause at the bottom. |
| Hips shoot up before your chest | Lead with your chest and drive through both heels equally. |
| Lower back rounds at the bottom | Stop a little higher. Work on ankle and hip mobility. |
| Looking up (at the mirror, say) or tucking your chin | Look straight ahead, or at a spot on the floor a few steps in front. Keep your neck long, in line with your spine. |

## Make it easier
| Step | What changes | Pick this if… |
|---|---|---|
| **Chair or Box Squat** (new) | Sit back until you lightly touch a chair or box, then stand. The seat shows you how deep to go. | You lose balance, or can't control the way down. |
| **Air Squat** (`ex_061`) | No weight. Arms out in front for balance. | You can sit to the chair easily and want to practise without a weight. |
| **Heel-Raised Goblet Squat** (new) | Same squat, with a small plate or board under both heels. | Your heels lift or you tip forward because your ankles are tight. |

## Make it harder
| Step | What changes | Pick this if… |
|---|---|---|
| **Pause Goblet Squat** (new) | Hold the bottom for 3 seconds each rep. | You can do 12 clean reps. |
| **Bulgarian Split Squat** (`ex_003`) | One foot behind you on a bench, squat on the front leg. | You can do 12 clean reps and want a one-leg challenge. |

## Safety
- Stop if you feel sharp pain, or pinching at the front of your hip.
- Take care if you have a knee, hip or lower back injury. Ask a doctor or physio before loading it.
- If you have high blood pressure or a heart condition, ask your doctor before bracing hard and holding your breath.

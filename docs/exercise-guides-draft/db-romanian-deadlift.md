# DB Romanian Deadlift — guide content (DRAFT, not reviewed by a trainer)

Catalogue entries this covers: `ex_048` DB Romanian Deadlift. (The barbell version, `ex_002`, has a different setup, so it would get its own guide later.)
Status: **draft**. Nothing here is shown to users until it is marked reviewed.
Items marked **[CONFIRM]** are choices where sources differ or you / a trainer should decide.

How it differs from the DB Deadlift (`ex_025`): the Romanian deadlift **starts standing**, the knees stay softly bent and almost still, and you lower only as far as your back stays flat, then stand back up. The weights never rest on the floor between reps. The deadlift starts from the floor and bends the knees more.

## Top of page
- **One-line summary:** Push your hips back, slide the dumbbells down your legs with a flat back until you feel your hamstrings stretch, then stand tall.
- **Level:** Beginner – Intermediate. Learn the hip hinge with no weight first.
- **Equipment:** Two dumbbells.
- **Main muscles:** Hamstrings, glutes.
- **Helping muscles:** Lower back (holds your position), upper back, core, forearms (grip).
- **Starting weight:** Light, a weight you can control for 8–12 reps. Add weight slowly once the hinge feels automatic.

## 3 key cues (the "Cues" card)
1. Soft knees, then push your hips back, as if closing a car door behind you with your bottom. It is a hinge, not a squat.
2. Weights close, back straight. Keep the dumbbells close to your legs and your back long and flat, from head to tailbone.
3. Hamstring stretch, then stand. Lower until you feel your hamstrings stretch, a few inches below your knee, then drive your hips forward and stand tall. Don't lean back at the top.

Cues 2 and 3 as worded by Amrit, 12 Nov 2026.

## Quick view
- **Breathing:** In and brace as you start to hinge, out as you stand. **[CONFIRM]** Same wording as the DB Deadlift guide; sources differ on exact breathing.
- **Tempo:** 2 seconds down, 1 second pause at the bottom, 1 second up.
- **Sets and reps:** 3 sets of 8–12.

## How low to go (the "Depth" card, titled "How low")
- **Goal:** Lower a few inches below your knee, until you feel a stretch in the back of your thighs, as long as your back stays flat. It depends on your hamstring mobility, so it is a goal, not a must.
- **Stop going down when:** your back starts to round, your knees start to bend a lot (it is turning into a squat), or the dumbbells drift away from your legs.
- **After the stop point:** That is your depth for today. Pause one second, then stand. Don't chase more depth.
- **Posture note:** Chest stays up and your core stays tight the whole way.
- **[CONFIRM] depth:** "a few inches below the knee" is Amrit's target. The sources I found give no exact depth, so a trainer should check it. The DB Deadlift guide uses "about knee height".

## Stance
Feet hip-width apart, toes pointing forward or turned out slightly, knees softly bent. Brace your core.

## How to do it
**Setup**
1. Stand tall holding a dumbbell in each hand in front of your thighs, palms facing your legs.
2. Feet hip-width apart, a soft bend in your knees. Keep that same bend the whole set.
3. Shoulders back and down, chest up, neck in line with your spine.
4. Breathe in and brace your core.

**Movement**
1. Push your hips straight back. Your knees bend only a little, and your shins stay almost upright.
2. Slide the dumbbells down your thighs and shins, keeping them close to your legs.
3. Stop a few inches below your knee, when you feel your hamstrings stretch or your back is about to round.
4. Drive your hips forward and stand tall. Squeeze your glutes at the top. Don't lean back.

## Common mistakes
| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| Knees bend a lot and it turns into a squat | Push your hips back first, and keep the same soft knee bend all the way down. |
| Back rounds | Stop higher. Keep your chest up and your back long from head to tailbone. |
| Dumbbells drift away from your legs | Keep them brushing against your thighs and shins. |
| Knees locked straight | Keep a soft bend, never locked. |
| Leaning back at the top | Stand tall and squeeze your glutes. Don't push your hips past straight. |
| Dropping the weights quickly | Take 2 seconds to lower them. |
| Looking up or tucking your chin | Look at the floor a few steps ahead. Keep your neck in line with your spine. |

## Make it easier
| Step | What changes | Pick this if… |
|---|---|---|
| **Wall Hip Hinge** | No weight. Stand a foot from a wall with your back to it, push your hips back until your bottom taps the wall, then stand. | You can't keep your back flat or feel it in your lower back. |
| **Lighter weight, same lift** | Drop weight until every rep is smooth. | Your back rounds or the weights drift away. |
| **KB Deadlift** (`ex_095`) | One weight held between your feet, starting from the floor. | Holding two dumbbells in front of you feels awkward. |

## Make it harder
| Step | What changes | Pick this if… |
|---|---|---|
| **Pause RDL** | Hold the bottom for 3 seconds each rep. | You can do 12 clean reps. |
| **Barbell Romanian Deadlift** (`ex_002`) | The same hinge with a barbell. It carries more weight. | You can do 12 clean reps and the dumbbells feel too light. |
| **Single-leg RDL** | One dumbbell, one leg. | You can do 12 clean reps and want a balance challenge. |

## Safety
- Stop if you feel sharp pain in your back, or pain running down a leg.
- If you've had back pain or an injury, ask a doctor or physio before loading this.
- Control the weights and don't drop them from height.
- With high blood pressure or a heart condition, ask your doctor before bracing hard and holding your breath.
